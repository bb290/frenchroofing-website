# French Roofing's style

California surfer vibe meets Pacific Northwest contractor: laid back, warm, neighborly, photo-forward. The videos are for homeowners in Damascus, Clackamas, Happy Valley and nearby who want a roofer they can trust, and they sound like Sean talking to a neighbor. They must never look corporate, high-pressure, flashy or like a generic contractor template. Built 2026-11-10 from `.claude/design-system/DESIGN.md` and `.claude/BRAND.md` (no inspiration videos yet), plus the look Brittany approved on the 2026-10-26 reel pilot ("What's in a new roof?").

## References
- `social/reels/2026-10-26-whats-in-a-new-roof/`: the approved pilot. Take its caption treatment (white, outlined, no box), yellow step chips on a navy pill, the cream end card with the logo, and the calm pace.

## Type
- Display: Inter 800, upright: hooks, thesis words, big figures. The brand is Inter for everything.
- Text: Inter 700: captions, labels, text boxes.
- Mono: Inter 600 (the brand has no mono; use tabular figures): numbers, units, kickers.
- Caveat, Permanent Marker and Shrikhand are for customer review graphics only. Never in reels.

## Colour
- Accent #FFBD59 (yellow) on navy and on footage. On the light cream ground the accent is orange #D85024, because yellow fails on cream (a deliberate variant, not drift).
- Negative #F2708A on dark, #9E1B3A on light (a berry red, moved off the brand's error red because it read too close to the orange accent). Positive #86B98A on dark, #4A7C4E on light.
- Grounds: light is cream #F5F1EA with navy ink #092E5E; dark is navy #092E5E with cream text. Flat (no grid), 8px corners.
- Never a gradient, glow or drop shadow on cards. The only shadow allowed is the caption outline on footage.

## Captions
- On footage: white Inter, a dark outline and soft shadow, no box (as the pilot). Normal case, a short phrase at a time (3 words, about 22 characters).
- Hi-fi captions sit in a navy box (88%) with white text.
- One emphasis word per line at most, in yellow.

## Motion
Calm. Photos hold long enough to see the roof. Gentle 3% pushes only: no big zooms, no whip pans, no pans across a photo. Landscape photos show whole, full width, with a blurred fill behind. No colour overlay on photos.

## Sound
Light effects, sitting under the voice. Music only when a track is handed over (the pilot used a soft instrumental bed at low volume).

## Hi-fi
A two-line hook: a plain line over a big display line. Captions cut in 2-4 word phrases. Type behind Sean's head sometimes, not every video. Full-screen cards sometimes, for a number or a list (layers of a roof, steps of a job), cream or navy, flat, 8px corners. The camera settles on each cut. No black and white.

## Lo-fi
Sean on his phone on the job. Captions a phrase at a time, low in the frame and clear of his face. Native-looking white text boxes with navy text for the hook and the CTA. Job photos and short clips often drop on top of him; backdrops behind his head now and then. No zooms.

## Showcase
Used sparingly; this brand is not flashy. The big word lands with a gentle scale-up, never a slam. Transitions: push, reveal, drop. Never spin or whip. Medium density. Motif: yellow step chips and job-site tape.

## Never
- No phone number anywhere on screen (social drives to frenchroofing.com, not calls). The CTA is "Get your Instant Roof Quote at French Roofing dot com" / the frenchroofing.com pill.
- Never say or show "family-owned" on social.
- Never show or say the street address. Never name Portland. Never name a city outside BRAND.md section 9.
- No house numbers, licence plates, identifiable home fronts or paperwork in shot.
- No crew on a roof without visible fall protection.
- No stock footage, no filters, no duotones, no generated imagery as the look of the video.
- No emoji, no gradients, no em dashes in on-screen text.
