# Claude cheat sheet for Sean

One rule: **do all French Roofing work inside the "French Roofing" project.** Open claude.ai, click Projects, click French Roofing, start a chat. That's where Claude knows your business, your voice, and your rules.

## Email

| Say this                                                 | What happens                                               |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Triage my inbox"                                        | Quick rundown of what needs you, leads first, junk skipped |
| "Catch me up on email since Monday"                      | Same thing for a longer window                             |
| "Draft a reply to \[name]"                               | Claude reads the thread and writes a reply in your voice   |
| "Follow up with everyone I sent estimates to last week"  | Follow-up drafts for each                                  |
| "Write an email telling \[name] we found rotten decking" | Bad-news email, straight talk plus options                 |

## The two things to remember

1. **Claude never sends anything.** Every email lands in your Gmail Drafts folder. You read it, fix anything, and hit send yourself.
2. **Brackets mean "fill me in."** If a draft says \[PRICE] or \[DAY/TIME], Claude didn't know that number and won't guess. Put in the real one before sending.

## Also handy

- "Summarize this thread" when a customer email chain gets long.
- "What did I tell \[name] about their warranty?" to check what you already promised.
- New lead came in? Claude will remind you to put it in Housecall Pro. It's right.
