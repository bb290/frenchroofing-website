**Subject:** You're on the list, [FIRST NAME]! Here's what happens next

Hi [FIRST NAME],

Thanks for choosing French Roofing for [PROJECT, e.g. your roof replacement]. We're glad to have you, and here's exactly what happens from here so you're never left wondering.

**1. Pay your deposit**
Your 50% deposit of [DEPOSIT AMOUNT] holds your spot on the schedule. You can pay here: [DEPOSIT LINK]. Check, ACH (bank transfer) and card all work. Card payments carry a 3% processing fee, so check or ACH saves you a little. If the amount is more than your bank allows in a day, just reply and we'll split it into smaller invoices.

**2. Pick your shingle color**
Browse the colors for [SHINGLE LINE] here: [MANUFACTURER COLOR PAGE]. Pop in your zip code to see what's available locally. Every color in the same line is the same price. If you'd rather see samples in person, let us know and we'll bring a sample board.

**3. We'll lock in your start date**
Once your deposit and color are in, we'll send your start date. Right now we're about 2 weeks out. If you've got a deadline (travel, a closing date, another contractor), tell us now and we'll plan around it.

**What install day looks like**
- The crew starts around 8am and usually wraps up by mid-afternoon.
- You don't need to be home. We just need access to the outside of the house.
- Please move cars out of the driveway before 8am, and take anything fragile off walls and shelves (the hammering can shake things).
- Most replacements take [TYPICAL DURATION] depending on size and weather.

**If it rains**
We watch the forecast closely and will reach out if we need to move your date. Once the new underlayment is down, your roof is water tight, so a passing shower mid-job isn't a problem.

**If we find damaged decking**
Your estimate includes [DECKING ALLOWANCE]. If we find more rot once the old roof is off, it's [PRICE PER SHEET] per sheet, and [CONFIRM: we'll call you with photos before going past the allowance]. If we use less than the allowance, you'll see the credit on your final invoice.

**When we're done**
We'll clean up the site, including a blower pass and cleaning out your gutters from the tear-off. You'll get your final invoice and photos of the finished roof by email, and your [5-YEAR for 30-year roofs / 10-YEAR for 50-year roofs] workmanship warranty with your closeout paperwork.

Questions anytime? Just reply to this email or call or text 971-376-8722. A real person reads every message.

Thanks,
Sean
French Roofing
