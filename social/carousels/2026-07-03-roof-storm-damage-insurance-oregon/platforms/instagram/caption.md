Storm got your roof? Take a breath. The steps from here are simpler than they feel.

The short version of our storm playbook:

• Stay off the roof. Bucket the drip, tarp it properly, keep every receipt
• Photos before cleanup, always. Wide shots, close-ups, and the branch that did it
• Get a real repair estimate BEFORE deciding whether to file a claim
• Your roofer documents and estimates. Anyone guaranteeing claim approval is a red flag
• Check from the driveway: missing shingles, creased tabs, granules at the downspout

Full homeowner's playbook is on the blog, and the roof assessment is free. We document everything with photos, whether or not you ever hire us.
