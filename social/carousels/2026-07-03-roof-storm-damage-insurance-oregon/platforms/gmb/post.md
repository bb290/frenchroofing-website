Storm damage on your roof? Here is the playbook we give every homeowner: stay off the roof and get it tarped properly, photograph everything before cleanup starts, and get a real repair estimate before deciding whether an insurance claim makes sense. Be wary of anyone who guarantees claim approval or offers to cover your deductible; that is a red flag, not a discount. We document storm damage with photos as part of a free roof assessment, and you keep the photos whether or not you hire us.
