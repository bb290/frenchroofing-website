Storm got your roof? Take a breath. The steps from here are simpler than they feel.

The short version of our storm playbook:

• Stay off the roof. Bucket the drip, tarp it properly, keep every receipt
• Photos before cleanup, always. The photos are the claim
• Get a real repair estimate BEFORE deciding whether to file
• Anyone guaranteeing claim approval or "covering your deductible" is a red flag
• Check from the driveway: missing shingles, creased tabs, granules at the downspout

The full homeowner's playbook is on our site, and the roof assessment is free. We document everything with photos, whether or not you ever hire us.
