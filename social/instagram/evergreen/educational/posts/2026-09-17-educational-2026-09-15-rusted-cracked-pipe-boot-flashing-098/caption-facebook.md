# Caption (facebook)

This little piece of rubber and metal is one of the most common reasons a roof leaks, and most homeowners have never heard of it.

It's a pipe boot. Every plumbing vent that pokes through your roof has one. The metal base seals against the shingles, and the rubber collar wraps tight around the pipe. When both are working, water runs right past. When they're not, it finds its way in.

The trouble is that rubber breaks down faster than shingles do. We see split collars on roofs that still have years of life left. The boot fails quietly, usually somewhere around year 10 to 15, and by the time a water stain shows up inside, it's been leaking for a while.

If your roof is more than a decade old and hasn't had an inspection, pipe boots are one of the first things we check. A failed one is usually a repair, not a full replacement. Worth knowing before a small fix turns into a bigger conversation.
