# Caption

This little piece of rubber and metal is one of the most common reasons a roof leaks, and most homeowners have never heard of it.

It's a pipe boot. Every plumbing vent that pokes through your roof has one. The metal base seals against the shingles, and the rubber collar wraps tight around the pipe. When both are working, water runs right past. When they're not, it finds its way in.

The problem is that rubber breaks down faster than shingles do. We see split collars on roofs that have years of life left. The boot fails quietly, usually around year 10 to 15, and by the time a stain shows up inside, water has been traveling in for a while.

If your roof is more than a decade old and hasn't had an inspection, pipe boots are one of the first things we look at. A failed one is usually a repair, not a replacement. Worth knowing before a small fix turns into a bigger one.
