# Caption (gmb)

Pipe boot failures are one of the most common sources of roof leaks we find during inspections in the Damascus area.

A pipe boot is the fitting that seals each plumbing vent where it exits your roof. It has a metal base and a rubber collar. The rubber breaks down faster than the shingles around it, so a boot can start leaking on a roof that still has years of life left.

The one in this photo had a rusting base and a collar splitting around the pipe. It was spotted during a routine inspection. Caught early, a failed pipe boot is typically a straightforward repair, not a full roof replacement.

If your roof is 10 years or older and hasn't been inspected recently, it's worth having someone take a look at the pipe boots, flashing, and other penetrations before the rainy season picks up.
