# Caption (gmb)

Roof inspection showing end-of-life three-tab shingles, tabs missing and the underlayer exposed.

This is a common finding on homes in the Damascus area with older three-tab roofs. Once tabs start breaking off, the surrounding shingles are usually just as worn and close behind. At that point, patching doesn't do much. The whole field is at the end of its service life.

Three-tab shingles are thinner than modern architectural shingles and wear faster under Pacific Northwest rain, moss, and freeze-thaw conditions. If your roof is original three-tab and has been up 20-plus years, a quick inspection before fall rains is a smart move.
