# Caption

A lot of people picture a roof repair as swapping out a shingle or two. What's actually happening is a little more involved than that.

On this Estacada job, we cut out the damaged section, pulled back to clean decking, and laid fresh synthetic underlayment before a single new shingle went down. The underlayment is the layer that actually keeps water out if a shingle ever lifts or cracks. If you skip it during a repair, you're just covering the problem.

The nail gun in the foreground isn't decoration. Proper fastening pattern and depth matter too. Too shallow and the shingles lift in wind. Too deep and the nail cuts right through the shingle.

A repair done right isn't faster than a replacement. It's just smaller. But it has to be built the same way.
