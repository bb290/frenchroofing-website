# Caption (gmb)

Roof repair in Estacada, OR: what's actually involved.

When French Roofing repairs a damaged section of an architectural shingle roof, we go all the way back to clean decking. That means cutting out the affected area, installing fresh synthetic underlayment, and then setting new shingles with the right fastening pattern and nail depth. The underlayment is what keeps water out when a shingle shifts or cracks. Skipping it during a repair just covers the problem instead of solving it.

This is standard practice on every repair we do in Estacada, Clackamas, Happy Valley, Damascus, and the surrounding area. A repair built to spec performs like a repair, not a temporary patch.
