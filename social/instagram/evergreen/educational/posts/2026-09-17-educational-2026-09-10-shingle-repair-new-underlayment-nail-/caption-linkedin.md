# Caption (linkedin)

When a repair comes up on an architectural shingle roof, there's a temptation to treat it as a surface fix. Pull the bad shingle, drop in a new one, done. That approach skips the part that actually matters.

On a recent Estacada job, we cut out the damaged section down to clean decking and installed fresh synthetic underlayment before any new shingles went in. The underlayment is the roof's real water barrier. If it's compromised and you leave it, you've just put new shingles over a problem that's still there.

Fastening pattern and nail depth matter just as much. Off-spec installation voids the manufacturer warranty on the repaired section, and that's a conversation nobody wants to have later.
