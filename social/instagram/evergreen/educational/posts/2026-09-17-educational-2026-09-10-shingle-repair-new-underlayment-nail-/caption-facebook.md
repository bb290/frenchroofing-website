# Caption (facebook)

A lot of people picture a roof repair as swapping out a shingle or two. What's actually happening is a little more involved than that.

On this Estacada job, we cut out the damaged section, pulled back to clean decking, and laid fresh synthetic underlayment before a single new shingle went down. The underlayment is the layer that actually keeps water out if a shingle ever lifts or cracks. Skip it during a repair and you're just covering the problem, not fixing it.

Proper fastening matters too. Too shallow and shingles lift in a windstorm. Too deep and the nail cuts right through the shingle. A repair done right is built the same way a full replacement is, just smaller in scope.
