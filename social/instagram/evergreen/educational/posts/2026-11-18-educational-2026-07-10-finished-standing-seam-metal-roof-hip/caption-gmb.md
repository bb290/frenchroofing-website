Standing seam metal roof replacement completed in Boring, OR.

Each panel on a standing seam system runs in one piece from ridge to eave. Where two panels meet, the edges lock together into that raised seam you see running down the slope. Water flows down the flat pans between the seams. Where two slopes meet at the hip, the panels are cut to meet along that line and a matching metal hip cap covers the joint.
