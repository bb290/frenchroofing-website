# Caption (gmb)

Roof replacement in the Damascus area, here's a look at what goes on before the shingles do.

After tear-off, the entire roof slope gets covered in synthetic underlayment, laid in horizontal courses starting at the gutter edge and working up. Each course overlaps the one below it so water sheds down and off the roof, not into the deck. It's a second line of defense: if water ever gets past the shingles, the underlayment stops it from reaching the wood underneath.

Most homeowners never see this layer. By the time we're done, it's hidden beneath the shingles. But it's one of the details worth asking about on any roof bid.
