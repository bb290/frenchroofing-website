Roof repair in Gresham, OR: no open roofs left overnight.

When a roof is mid-replacement or mid-repair, the deck can be exposed at the end of a work day. Our crew lays underlayment over every opening and weights it down with boards and bricks before leaving the job site. This temporary dry-in keeps the interior protected if rain moves through overnight.

It's a standard step on every French Roofing job. The homeowner may never see it, but it's part of how we run a clean, accountable job site from day one to final nail.
