# Caption (instagram)

An open roof never spends the night that way.

When we pull shingles and expose the deck mid-job, the house isn't done for the day just because the crew is. Before anyone packs up, we lay underlayment over the opening and weight it down with boards and bricks. It's not pretty, but it's solid. If rain rolls through Gresham overnight, the inside of that house stays dry.

We call it a dry-in. It's one of those things that's easy to skip if you're in a hurry, and the homeowner would never know until water showed up on their ceiling. So we don't skip it.

Every opening gets covered. Every night. That's just how a job site should run.
