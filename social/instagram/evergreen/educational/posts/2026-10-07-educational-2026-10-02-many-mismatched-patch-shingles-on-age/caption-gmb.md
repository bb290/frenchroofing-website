# Caption (gmb)

Roof repair vs. replacement assessment in Gladstone, OR.

One or two patches on a roof is normal, and a good repair can extend a roof's life. But when patches start to outnumber the problems they fixed, the pattern tells a different story. We recently visited a Gladstone roof for an estimate and counted a dozen or more mismatched replacement shingles across the slope. Replacement shingles never match an aged roof exactly, and that is fine. The pattern is the information: each patch points to surrounding shingles that were already wearing when the repair went in.

At some point the honest conversation shifts from "what do we fix next" to whether the next repair is money well spent or whether it is time to look at the whole roof.
