# Caption (linkedin)

One or two patches on a roof is completely normal. A well-timed repair can add real life to a roof and delay a full replacement. But when the patches start to outnumber the problems they fixed, the pattern itself becomes the diagnostic.

We were on a roof in Gladstone recently for an estimate. A dozen or more mismatched replacement shingles scattered across the slope, different shades and textures, set in at different heights. Replacement shingles never match an aged roof exactly, and that is not the point. The point is that each patch usually means the surrounding shingles were already brittle and losing granules when it went in.

For property managers and owners weighing repair costs against long-term budgets, this is the inflection point worth catching early. The honest question is whether the next repair is money well spent or whether a full assessment makes more sense before another invoice arrives.
