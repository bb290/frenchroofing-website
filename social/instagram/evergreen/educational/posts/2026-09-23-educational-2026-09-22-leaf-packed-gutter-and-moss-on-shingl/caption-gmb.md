# Caption (gmb)

Fall roof maintenance in the Damascus area: clear gutters before the rain hits.

When gutters pack up with leaves and fir needles, rainwater overflows at the eave and sits at the shingle edge instead of draining away. At the same time, moss growing along shingle joints holds moisture against the roof surface every time it gets wet.

Neither issue is an emergency when you catch it in early fall. Both get more expensive if you wait until mid-winter to notice them.
