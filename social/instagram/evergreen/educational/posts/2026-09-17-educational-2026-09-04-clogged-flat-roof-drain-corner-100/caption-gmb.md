# Caption (gmb)

Flat roof drain maintenance in Troutdale, OR.

We were on a commercial flat roof in Troutdale recently for an inspection, and the corner drain was completely buried under dirt and debris. The staining up the parapet walls told the story: water had been standing on that roof well above where it should ever be.

Standing water adds weight, wears out roofing membrane faster, and eventually works its way into seams and penetrations. It's a common problem on flat and low-slope roofs, and it's usually a maintenance issue, not a full replacement.

If you own or manage a commercial or multi-family building with a flat roof in the Troutdale, Damascus, or Clackamas area, a quick drain inspection before fall rain season can save you from a much bigger problem later.
