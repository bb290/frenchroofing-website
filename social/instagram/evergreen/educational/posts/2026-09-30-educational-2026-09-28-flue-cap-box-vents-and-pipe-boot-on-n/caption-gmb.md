# Caption (gmb)

Every vent, flue, and pipe on a roof is a hole through it, and the flashing or boot around each one is what keeps that hole dry.

When we inspect a roof, penetrations are where we look first. Square box vents let attic heat and moisture out. Round metal caps with storm collars are appliance flues for furnaces or water heaters. The small black boot mid-slope covers the plumbing vent stack. Each one is a separate system with its own way of failing.

A loose storm collar, a cracked rubber boot, or a vent flange laid over the shingles instead of tucked under them on the uphill side can cause a leak that shows up on a ceiling nowhere near the vent. Hardly any of it shows from the ground.

If you've had a leak and aren't sure where it's coming from, a closer look at your penetrations is a good place to start.
