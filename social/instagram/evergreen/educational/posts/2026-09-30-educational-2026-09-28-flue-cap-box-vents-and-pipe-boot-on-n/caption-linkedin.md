# Caption (linkedin)

Every vent, flue, and pipe on a roof is a hole through it. The metal flashing or rubber boot around each one is what keeps that hole dry.

When we inspect a roof, penetrations are where we go first. Square box vents exhaust attic heat and moisture. Round metal caps with storm collars are appliance flues (furnace or water heater). The small black boot handles the plumbing vent stack. Each is a separate system, and each has its own failure mode.

A loose storm collar, a cracked rubber boot, a vent flange laid over the shingles instead of tucked under them on the uphill side. Hardly any of it shows from the ground. But these details account for a large share of the leaks we trace after heavy rain.

For property managers and building owners, this is worth knowing: a roof inspection that doesn't look at every penetration isn't a complete inspection. It's where we always start.
