# Caption (instagram)

Every vent, flue, and pipe on a roof is a hole through it. The metal flashing or rubber boot around each one is what keeps that hole dry.

When we look at a roof, those penetrations are where we go first. The square black box vents let attic heat and moisture escape. The round metal caps with the storm collars are appliance flues, furnace or water heater. The small black boot mid-slope is a plumbing vent. Each one is a separate system, and each one has its own way of failing.

A loose storm collar, a cracked rubber boot, a vent flange laid on top of the shingles instead of tucked under them on the uphill side. None of it looks dramatic from the ground. But up close, these are the spots that account for a big share of the leaks we trace back after a hard rain.

If someone tells you your roof has a leak, this is where we start looking before we look anywhere else.
