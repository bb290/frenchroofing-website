# Caption (instagram)

See those box vents lined up near the ridge? They're doing more work than most homeowners realize.

A hip roof like this one moves air differently than a gable roof. Without ridge vents running the full peak, box vents are what's carrying the exhaust load. The spacing and count matter. Too few vents and your attic traps heat in summer, moisture in winter, and your shingles age faster than they should from the underside out.

The brick chimney in the mix adds another variable. Chimney flashing is one of the more common spots we trace leaks back to, especially on older brick. Counter flashing, step flashing, mortar condition, it all gets checked when we're reading a roof like this one.

When we do an inspection, the ventilation system and the chimney both get eyes on them, not just the shingles. The shingles are often the last thing to show a problem.
