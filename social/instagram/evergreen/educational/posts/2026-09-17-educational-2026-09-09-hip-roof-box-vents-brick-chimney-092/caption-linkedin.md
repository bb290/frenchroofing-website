# Caption (linkedin)

See those box vents near the ridge? On a hip roof, they're carrying the full ventilation load since there's no continuous ridge vent running the peak. Spacing and count matter more than most people expect. Under-ventilated attics trap heat in summer and moisture in winter, which accelerates shingle wear from the inside out and can quietly void a manufacturer warranty.

The brick chimney is the other variable worth attention. Chimney flashing, counter flashing, step flashing, mortar condition, these are the details that show up on an inspection report and get missed when someone's only looking at shingle surface.

For property managers and building owners, a thorough inspection means looking at the whole system: shingles, ventilation, flashing, penetrations. That's what we document when we go out on an estimate.
