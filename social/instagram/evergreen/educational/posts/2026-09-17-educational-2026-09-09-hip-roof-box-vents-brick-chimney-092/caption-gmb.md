# Caption (gmb)

Roof ventilation and chimney flashing are two of the most overlooked parts of a roof system, and two of the most common sources of problems we find on inspections in the Damascus area.

On a hip roof, box vents near the ridge handle the full exhaust load. If there aren't enough of them, or they're not spaced right, the attic holds heat and moisture longer than it should. That shortens shingle life and can create conditions that quietly void a manufacturer warranty.

Brick chimneys add their own inspection points: counter flashing, step flashing, and mortar condition all factor into whether water is getting in, and from where.

A good roof inspection looks at the whole system, not just the surface. If your roof has box vents, a chimney, or both, and hasn't had a professional set of eyes on it recently, it's worth scheduling.
