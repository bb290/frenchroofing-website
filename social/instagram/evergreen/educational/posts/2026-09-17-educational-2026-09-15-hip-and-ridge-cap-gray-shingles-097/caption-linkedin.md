# Caption (linkedin)

Most homeowners know what a roof looks like. Not as many know what the details are actually doing.

The hip and ridge cap lines on an architectural shingle roof aren't decorative. They're the most exposed points on the whole system, taking the first hit from wind and water. Purpose-built ridge cap shingles are thicker and pre-bent to flex through temperature swings without cracking. It's a small spec detail that makes a real difference in how long the ridge holds up.

The plumbing vent boot in this photo is worth noting too. That boot seal is one of the most common sources of slow, hard-to-trace leaks we find during inspections and estimates.

For property managers and GCs, these are the details that separate a roof that goes the distance from one that starts causing problems early. We've been reading roofs in the Damascus area for 10+ years, and we're CertainTeed Certified (CCB #203933) for the installs that follow.
