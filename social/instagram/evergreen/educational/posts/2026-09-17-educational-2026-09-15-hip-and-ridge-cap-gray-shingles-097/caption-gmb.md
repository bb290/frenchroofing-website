# Caption (gmb)

Hip and ridge cap shingles: what they do and why they matter.

The ridge cap lines on an architectural shingle roof aren't just finishing trim. They're the most exposed points on the system, the spots most vulnerable to wind uplift and water intrusion. Purpose-built ridge cap shingles are thicker and pre-bent to flex with the roof deck through seasonal temperature changes without cracking. It's the kind of spec detail that separates a roof that holds up for decades from one that starts showing problems too soon.

That plumbing vent boot is worth watching too. The boot seal around penetrations like that is one of the most common spots we trace slow leaks back to during inspections.
