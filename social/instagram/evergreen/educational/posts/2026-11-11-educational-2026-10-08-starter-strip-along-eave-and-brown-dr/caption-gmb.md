# Caption (gmb)

Roof replacement in Gresham, OR: starter strip and drip edge installation.

Before the first full row of shingles goes on, two details have to be right. The drip edge is the brown metal along the roof edge in this photo. It gives water a clean edge to drip off instead of letting it curl back onto the fascia. The starter strip is the green piece with the dashed sealant lines. It runs along the edge under the first row of shingles, holds that row's bottom edge down, and covers the gaps between those first shingles.

Neither one is visible when the job wraps up, so they're worth asking about on any roof bid.
