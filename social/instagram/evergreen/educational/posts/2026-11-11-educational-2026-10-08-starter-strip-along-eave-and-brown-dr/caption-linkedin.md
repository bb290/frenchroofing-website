# Caption (linkedin)

Before the first full row of shingles goes on, two details have to be in place: drip edge and starter strip.

This is from a Gresham roof replacement we completed recently, photographed partway through. The starter strip, the green piece with the dashed sealant lines, runs along the edge under the first shingle course, holds that course's bottom edge down, and covers the gaps between the first shingles. The brown metal along the front edge is the drip edge, which lets water drip clear of the roof instead of curling back onto the fascia board.

Neither one is visible when the job is finished, which is exactly why they're worth asking about. For property managers and GCs, these are the details worth checking on any bid.
