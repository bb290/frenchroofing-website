# Caption (facebook)

Two things go on before the first full row of shingles: drip edge and starter strip.

You can see both here, partway through a roof replacement we did in Gresham. The starter strip is that green piece with the dashed sealant lines sitting on the underlayment. It runs along the edge under the first row of shingles, holds that row's bottom edge down, and covers the gaps between those first shingles so water can't sneak under at the edge.

The brown metal along the front edge is the drip edge. It gives water a clean edge to drip off, so it falls away from the roof instead of curling back onto the fascia board.

Neither of these shows once the job is done. That's kind of the point.
