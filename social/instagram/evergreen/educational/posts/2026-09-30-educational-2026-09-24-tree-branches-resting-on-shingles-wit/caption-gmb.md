# Caption (gmb)

Roof maintenance tip for Gresham homeowners: keep tree branches trimmed back off the roof before fall rains arrive.

On a recent inspection visit in Gresham, we saw a large birch with branches resting directly on the shingles near the ridge. Seed and leaf litter had collected along the eave, thin moss lines were already starting along the shingle joints, and those branches were in a position to scrub granules off the shingles every time the wind picked up.

Granules protect the asphalt from UV and rain. Litter holds moisture on the shingles and feeds moss growth. Branches hanging over the roof keep that section shaded and slow to dry. Trimming them back so they clear the roof is a simple maintenance step that can make a real difference over time.
