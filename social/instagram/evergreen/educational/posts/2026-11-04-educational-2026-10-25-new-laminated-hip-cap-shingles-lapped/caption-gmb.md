# Caption (gmb)

Oregon City roof replacement, a detail worth knowing about.

Where two roof faces meet along an angled hip, regular field shingles can't seal the seam. That's what hip cap shingles are for. Each cap bends over the hip and overlaps the one below it, covering the fasteners underneath. It's a straightforward detail, but it matters: hips and ridges are some of the most wind-exposed parts of a roof.

After a windstorm, these are the spots to check first. A lifted, cracked, or missing cap leaves that seam open to water.

We replaced this roof in Oregon City, and you can see the hip caps stepping along the hip in the photo.
