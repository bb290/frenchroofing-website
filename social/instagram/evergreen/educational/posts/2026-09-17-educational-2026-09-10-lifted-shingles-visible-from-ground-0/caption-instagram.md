# Caption (instagram)

After a big windstorm, it's worth taking a few minutes to look at your roof from the driveway. You don't need to climb up there. Just step back and look at the upper slopes near the vents and ridgeline.

This roof in Estacada is a good example of what to watch for. A few shingles lifted and shifted out of place, visible from the ground if you know what you're looking for. The tabs separate from the row below, or a corner kicks up and catches the light differently than the rest of the slope.

You don't always need a ladder to know something's off. A lifted shingle that's been sitting exposed through a few rain cycles can let water underneath before any ceiling stain shows up inside.

After a heavy wind event, walk your property and look up. If something looks off, even if you're not sure, that's worth a call before the next rain.
