# Caption (facebook)

After a big windstorm, it's worth taking a few minutes to look at your roof from the driveway. You don't need to climb up there. Just step back and scan the upper slopes near the vents and ridgeline.

This roof in Estacada is a good example of what to watch for. A few shingles lifted and shifted out of place, and you can spot them from the ground if you know what you're looking for. The tabs separate from the row below, or a corner kicks up and catches the light differently than the rest of the slope.

A lifted shingle that sits exposed through a few rain cycles can let water underneath before any ceiling stain ever shows up inside. So the earlier you catch it, the simpler the fix.

After a heavy wind event, just walk your property and look up. If something looks off, that's worth a call before the next rain hits.
