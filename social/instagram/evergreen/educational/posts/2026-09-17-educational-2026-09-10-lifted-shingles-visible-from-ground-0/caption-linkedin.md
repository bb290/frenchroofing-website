# Caption (linkedin)

Most homeowners don't think to check their roof after a windstorm unless there's already a leak. By then, the window for a simple repair has often closed.

This photo from a job in Estacada shows what displaced shingles look like from the ground. A few shingles lifted and shifted near the vents along the upper slope, visible without a ladder if you step back and look. Tabs separate from the course below, or a corner kicks up and reflects light differently than the surrounding field.

For property managers and GCs overseeing residential work, a post-storm visual check is a low-effort step that can prevent a small repair from turning into a full section replacement. We've been on roofs in the Damascus area for 10+ years and the pattern holds: the earlier the call, the simpler the fix.
