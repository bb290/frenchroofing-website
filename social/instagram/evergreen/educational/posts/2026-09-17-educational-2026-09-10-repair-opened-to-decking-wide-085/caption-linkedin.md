# Caption (linkedin)

This repair photo from Estacada is a good example of what separates a real fix from a patch job.

When we open a shingle repair, we pull everything down to the OSB decking. Not just the damaged shingles. The underlayment too. Because if the deck beneath has softened, rotted, or delaminated, covering it back up with new material just delays the next call, and the next repair is usually bigger.

Stopping short is faster and cheaper in the short run. But for property managers and building owners carrying multiple roofs, a buried problem is a liability, not a savings.

We've been doing this work in the Damascus area since 2014, CCB #203933, CertainTeed Certified. Every repair we touch gets opened far enough to know what we're working with.
