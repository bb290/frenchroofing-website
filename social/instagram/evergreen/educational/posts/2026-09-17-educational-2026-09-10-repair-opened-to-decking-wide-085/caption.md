# Caption

When we do a shingle repair, we go all the way down.

This one in Estacada shows what a proper repair actually looks like opened up. The damaged shingles come off, the underlayment comes off, and we get eyes on the OSB decking beneath. If the deck is soft, spongy, or showing rot, we deal with it now, not six months from now when the stain shows up on the ceiling.

A lot of repair work stops short of this. Slap a patch over the problem and move on. The issue with that is you've just buried the damage instead of fixing it. Water doesn't care if there's a new shingle on top.

If we're opening a repair, we're opening it all the way to make sure what's underneath is solid before anything goes back on.
