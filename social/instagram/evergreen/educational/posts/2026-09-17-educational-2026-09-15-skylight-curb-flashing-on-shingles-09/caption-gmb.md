# Caption (gmb)

Skylight flashing is one of the more common sources of roof leaks in the Damascus area, and it's easy to miss until the damage is already showing up inside.

A curb-mounted skylight depends on step flashing, counter-flashing, and clean shingle integration to stay watertight. When any of that shifts, cracks, or was never seated right to begin with, water gets in slowly and quietly. The drip might not appear until days after the storm.

During a roof inspection, we check the full flashing system around any skylights: gaps at the curb, lifted counter-flashing, worn sealant at the corners, and shingle condition around the frame. On skylights that are 15 to 20 years old, we'll also give you an honest read on whether a repair makes sense or whether planning a replacement with the next roof is the smarter move.
