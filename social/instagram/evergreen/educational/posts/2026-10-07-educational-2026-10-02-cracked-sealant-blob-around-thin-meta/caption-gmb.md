# Caption (gmb)

Roof inspection in Gladstone, OR: sealant is not flashing.

During a recent estimate visit in Gladstone, we came across a metal pipe penetrating an asphalt shingle roof with no metal flashing collar and no rubber boot around it. Instead, roofing mastic had been troweled around the base of the pipe where it meets the shingles. That sealant had cracked open on the downhill side, leaving a gap between the caulk and the pipe.

A properly detailed penetration gets a metal flashing collar or rubber boot with a flange that tucks under the shingles on the uphill side. Water sheds over it naturally. Sealant applied on top of the shingles has nothing to lap under, it dries out and cracks through PNW freeze-thaw cycles, and once it splits there is an open path for water to run down the outside of the pipe into the roof deck.

Caulk can be a short-term maintenance patch, but it is not a lasting solution for a roof penetration. If your roof has any pipes or similar features sealed only with caulk, it is worth having a licensed roofer take a look.
