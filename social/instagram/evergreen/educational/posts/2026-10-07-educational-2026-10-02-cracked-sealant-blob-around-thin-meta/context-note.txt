Teachable point a roofer would sign: sealant is not flashing. A proper penetration gets a metal flashing or a rubber boot whose flange tucks under the shingles on the uphill side so water is shed over it; a bead or mound of caulk on top of the shingles has nothing to lap under, it dries out and cracks within a few seasons of sun and freeze-thaw, and once it splits there is an open path for water to run down the outside of the pipe into the roof.
