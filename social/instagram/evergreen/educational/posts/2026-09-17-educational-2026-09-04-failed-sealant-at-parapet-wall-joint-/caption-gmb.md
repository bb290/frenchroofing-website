# Caption (gmb)

Roof maintenance in Troutdale: failed sealant at a parapet wall joint.

During a recent inspection in Troutdale, we found cracked and pulled-away sealant where a metal parapet cap meets a stucco wall. Visible gaps, no protection, and a water intrusion problem waiting to happen.

Parapet wall joints are high-movement areas. Thermal expansion and contraction work on sealant constantly, and standard caulk has a limited lifespan. When it fails and goes unnoticed, water finds its way in and the repair gets a lot more involved.

The fix is straightforward: inspect these joints regularly and reseal on a maintenance schedule. Catching it during an inspection like this one is a small repair. Finding it after a leak has traveled through a wall is not.
