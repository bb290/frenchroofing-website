# Caption (facebook)

Flashing is one of those details that's easy to overlook because it's usually buried under siding. But this is exactly where leaks start if it's done wrong.

This is roof-to-wall flashing on an addition we worked on in Gresham. The siding hadn't gone on yet when we took this photo, so you can see what's actually happening at that joint. Step flashing pieces overlap as they climb the wall, each one tucked under the course above it. Done right, water has nowhere to go but down and away.

When we get a call about a leak along a sidewall, this is the first place we look. Nine times out of ten, the flashing was either skipped, cut too short, or installed after the siding instead of woven with it.

If you're adding onto your home, make sure your roofer and your siding crew are talking before either one finishes their work. That handoff between the two trades is where this detail either gets done right or gets missed entirely.
