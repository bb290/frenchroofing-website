# Caption (gmb)

Roof-to-wall flashing done right in Gresham, OR.

This photo is from a residential addition we completed in Gresham. With the siding not yet installed, you can see the step flashing running up the slope against the new plywood sheathing. Each piece is overlapped and set in sequence so water drains down and away from the wall, not into it.

This is one of the most common sources of sidewall leaks we see in the Damascus and Gresham area. Flashing that was skipped, undersized, or installed out of order shows up eventually, usually after the siding is long finished and the repair is more involved than it needed to be.
