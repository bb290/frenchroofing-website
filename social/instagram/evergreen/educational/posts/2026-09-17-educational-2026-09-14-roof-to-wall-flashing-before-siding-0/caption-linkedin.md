# Caption (linkedin)

Flashing is one of those details that property managers and GCs sometimes don't think about until a leak shows up. By then, it's behind finished siding and twice the work to fix.

This photo is from a Gresham addition we completed in September. The siding wasn't on yet, so you can see the roof-to-wall step flashing running up the slope against the new sheathing. Each piece overlaps the one below, woven with the courses as they climb. That's the spec. Skip it or do it out of sequence and you've built a water pathway straight into the wall cavity.

When we're brought in as a sub on additions and remodels, this is the coordination point we flag early. The roofing trade and the siding trade have to sequence correctly. That conversation needs to happen before either crew wraps up their work.
