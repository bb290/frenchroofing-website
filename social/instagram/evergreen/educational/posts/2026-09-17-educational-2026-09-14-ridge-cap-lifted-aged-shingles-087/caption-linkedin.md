# Caption (linkedin)

We pulled up a ridge cap on a Gresham roof this month and found aged architectural shingles with a failed sealant bond and enough lift to leave the peak exposed to wind and water. The homeowner had no idea the ridge was the issue until we traced it.

Ridge caps are the hardest-working part of a roof system. They catch wind from both sides, take concentrated UV exposure, and are the first thing to go when shingles age past their service life. When they start lifting, the opening is at the highest point of the roof, which means water has the easiest possible path in.

For property managers and building owners, this is worth knowing: a failing ridge cap doesn't always mean a full replacement. A targeted repair can extend a roof's life if the field shingles still have integrity. The diagnostic matters.
