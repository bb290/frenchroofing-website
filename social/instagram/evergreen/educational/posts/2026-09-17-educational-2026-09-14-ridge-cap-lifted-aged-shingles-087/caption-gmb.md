# Caption (gmb)

Roof repair in Gresham, OR: lifted and failing ridge cap on aging architectural shingles.

We recently serviced a Gresham roof where the ridge cap had lifted and the sealant strip on the underlying shingles had broken down completely. From the street it looked like a normal older roof. Up close, the peak was open to wind and rain.

Ridge caps wear faster than field shingles because they sit at the highest point, catch wind from both directions, and take more direct sun. Once they start lifting, water has a direct path into the roof system right at the top.

The good news: a failing ridge cap doesn't always mean a full replacement. If the field shingles still have life in them, a targeted ridge repair can buy several more years. The key is getting a proper assessment so you know what you're actually dealing with.
