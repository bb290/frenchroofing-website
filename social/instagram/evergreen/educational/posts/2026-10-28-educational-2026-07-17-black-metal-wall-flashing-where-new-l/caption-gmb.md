# Caption (gmb)

Roof-to-wall flashing on a Fairview roof replacement by French Roofing.

Wherever a roof surface meets a wall is one of the first places water finds a way in if the detail isn't correct. Roofing alone doesn't seal that transition. The solution is metal flashing that lies flat on the roof and turns up against the wall, with the siding lapping over the top. Water running down the wall lands on the flashing and drains out onto the roof, never behind it.

On this completed roof replacement in Fairview, the flashing follows the wall base through an inside corner and an outside corner, keeping the full jog covered. Corners are where these details get tested.

If you have a low-slope or flat roof that meets a wall and you're not sure whether the flashing is right, a quick inspection can answer that before a small gap becomes a bigger repair.
