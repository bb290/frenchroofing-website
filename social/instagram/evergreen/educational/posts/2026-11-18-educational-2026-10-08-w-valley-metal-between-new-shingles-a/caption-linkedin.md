# Caption (linkedin)

That brown metal strip running between two roof planes is a W-valley, a metal valley, on a Gresham roof replacement we completed this fall.

Valleys are where two slopes meet, so they carry the water from both. A metal valley lets that water flow off easily, collects less debris, and is easier to clean and maintain. Metal is also long-lived, and it's considered the premium choice for valleys. On a CertainTeed Presidential Shake roof like this one, metal valleys are required.

For property managers and GCs comparing roofing scopes, valley treatment is worth asking about on any bid.
