# Caption (facebook)

That brown metal strip running down the valley between the two roof planes is a W-valley, and it's worth knowing what it does.

Where two slopes meet, all the water from both of them runs down that one line. A metal valley lets it flow off the roof easily, less builds up in it, and when something does, it's easier to clean out. Metal valleys can also outlast just about everything else on a roof.

On a CertainTeed Presidential Shake roof like this one we did in Gresham, metal valleys aren't optional. You have to use metal. But even on roofs where you have a choice, metal is the top-tier pick. It's easier to maintain, and it looks better too.

If you've got a valley on your roof, it's worth knowing what's in it.
