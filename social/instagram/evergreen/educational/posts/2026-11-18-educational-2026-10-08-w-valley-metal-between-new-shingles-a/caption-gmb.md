# Caption (gmb)

Roof repair and replacement in Gresham, OR.

The brown metal strip you see running between two roof slopes on a job like this is called a W-valley, or metal valley. It sits where two roof planes meet and lets the water from both flow off the roof easily. Less debris builds up, and when it does, it's easier to clean out. On a CertainTeed Presidential Shake roof like this one, metal valleys are required. Even when they're optional, metal is considered the top-tier choice for valleys.
