# Caption (gmb)

Roof-to-wall flashing in Gresham, OR. This is what proper installation looks like at the junction where an existing shingle roof meets the fresh plywood wall of a new addition. Wall flashing and membrane layered correctly before siding covers the joint.

This detail is one of the most common sources of leaks on additions and remodels in the area. When it's skipped or rushed, water works into the wall assembly and the damage shows up as an interior stain, sometimes years later, long after siding has buried the problem.

French Roofing serves Gresham, Damascus, Clackamas, Happy Valley, and the surrounding communities. We do this work on new construction tie-ins, additions, and remodels, and we do it the same way whether it's going to be seen or not.
