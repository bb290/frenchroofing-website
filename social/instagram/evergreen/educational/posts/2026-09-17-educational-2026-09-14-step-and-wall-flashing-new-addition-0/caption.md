# Caption

The spot where a roof meets a wall is one of the most common places we find leaks. Not because it's hard to do right, but because it's easy to skip when nobody's watching.

This is the wall flashing on a Gresham addition, installed before the siding goes on. Black flashing and membrane layered at the roof-to-wall joint so water has nowhere to sneak in. Once the siding covers it, you'll never see it again. That's kind of the point.

If it's done wrong under there, the first sign is usually a stain on the interior wall, months or years later, after the water's had plenty of time to do its thing. By then the repair is a lot bigger than the original flashing job.

When we're tying into an existing roof for an addition, this joint gets the same attention as everything visible. Because what's hidden is what matters most.
