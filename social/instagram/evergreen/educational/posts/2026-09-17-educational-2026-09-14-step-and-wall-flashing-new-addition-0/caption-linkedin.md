# Caption (linkedin)

The roof-to-wall joint is one of the most common leak points we see on additions and remodels. Not because it's complicated, but because it gets covered up by siding and nobody checks it again for years.

This is the wall flashing installed on a Gresham addition before the siding went on. Black flashing and membrane layered at the joint so water has a clear path off the roof and away from the wall assembly. Once the siding is up, this detail is invisible. That's exactly why it has to be right before it disappears.

When the repair finally shows up, it's usually a stain on an interior wall, and by then the damage has spread well past the original flashing. A much bigger fix than doing it correctly the first time.
