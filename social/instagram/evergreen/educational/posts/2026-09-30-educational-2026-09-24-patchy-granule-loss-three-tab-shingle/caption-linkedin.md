# Caption (linkedin)

Granules are the part of a shingle that most property owners never think about until something goes wrong. They're the gritty surface coating, and their job is to protect the asphalt layer underneath from UV exposure and weather. When they wear away, the asphalt breaks down and the shingle wears out much faster.

We inspected a roof in Gresham recently and photographed this close-up of three-tab shingles with patchy granule loss. Some tabs still hold decent coverage. Others have gone darker where bare asphalt is showing through. Along the bottom edges of several tabs, the fiberglass mat is starting to emerge, pale and frayed at the edge.

The spread-out, tab-by-tab pattern is the diagnostic signal here. Localized granule loss can have a simple cause. When it's uneven across the whole slope, that usually points to gradual wear across the surface rather than a single event.
