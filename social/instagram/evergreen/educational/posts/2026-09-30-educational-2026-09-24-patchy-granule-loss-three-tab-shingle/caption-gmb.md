# Caption (gmb)

Roof inspection in Gresham, OR: what patchy granule loss actually looks like.

Granules are the gritty surface coating on asphalt shingles. Their job is to protect the asphalt underneath from UV and weather. When they wear away unevenly, some tabs go darker where bare asphalt is exposed, and the fiberglass mat can start to show along the bottom edges of the tab, pale and frayed-looking.

That tab-by-tab, uneven pattern is the thing to pay attention to. It usually means gradual surface wear across the slope rather than damage from a single event.

This kind of granule loss doesn't automatically mean a roof needs replacing, but it does mean it's worth having a roofer take a look. A quick inspection tells you where you actually stand, and that's a lot better than finding out through a ceiling stain.
