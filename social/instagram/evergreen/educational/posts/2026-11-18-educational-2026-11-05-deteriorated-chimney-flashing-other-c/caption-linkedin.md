# Caption (linkedin)

We came out to a home in Sandy, OR on an inspection request for a skylight leak. The roof had been installed by another contractor. When the homeowner went to use the workmanship warranty, they found that company was out of business and couldn't be reached. The photo is the chimney flashing on that roof.

A workmanship warranty is only as good as the company standing behind it.

Two things property owners and managers can do to protect themselves: get the roof registered with the manufacturer, which backs up the installer's workmanship warranty, and check how long a roofing company has been in business before signing. A company that's been around more than 5 years is far more likely to still be there 5 or 10 years from now.
