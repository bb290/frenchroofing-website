# Caption (instagram)

We got called out to a home in Sandy to look at a skylight leak. The roof had been put on by another company, and when the homeowner went to use their workmanship warranty, they found out that company was out of business and couldn't be reached. This photo is the chimney flashing on that same roof.

That's a tough spot to be in, and it's not the homeowner's fault. Two things we tell people so they don't end up there:

Get your new roof registered with the manufacturer. That gives you backup behind the installer's workmanship warranty.

Hire a roofer that's been in business more than 5 years. A company with some history is a lot more likely to still be around 5 or 10 years from now, when you might need them.

Worth asking about both before you sign anything.
