# Caption (gmb)

Roof inspection in Sandy, OR, and a lesson about roof warranties.

We came out to a home in Sandy on an inspection request for a skylight leak. The roof had been installed by another company, and when the homeowner went to use the workmanship warranty, that company was out of business and couldn't be reached. The photo is the chimney flashing on that roof.

Two things worth doing when you get a new roof. First, get it registered with the manufacturer, which backs up the installer's workmanship warranty. Second, hire a roofer that's been in business more than 5 years, so they're likely to still be around 5 or 10 years from now.
