# Caption (linkedin)

Here's something most property owners never see: a cross-section of a real roof system.

This is a square opening through an older roof in Gresham, from a recent job. From the top down, you've got the shingle, the felt underlayment, and the wood deck. Three layers, each with a role.

The shingle sheds water. The felt is the backup when water finds a gap. The deck carries structural load and anchors every nail. When one layer is compromised, the others absorb the slack, and that's when deferred maintenance turns into a bigger repair bill.

For property managers and GCs, understanding the system matters. A roof inspection that only looks at the surface shingles misses the layers below. We've been doing this for 10+ years in the Damascus area (CCB #203933, CertainTeed Certified), and the detail work is where it counts.
