# Caption (facebook)

Curious what's actually under your shingles? Here's a cross-section most homeowners never get to see.

This is a square opening in an older roof in Gresham. Top layer is the shingle, below that is the felt underlayment, and underneath that is the wood deck. Three layers between you and the rain, and each one has a specific job.

The shingle sheds water. The felt catches what sneaks through. The deck carries the load and gives the nails something to hold. When one layer fails, the others start working overtime, and that's usually when we get the call.

If you've ever wondered why we talk so much about what's underneath, this is why. A roof is a system, not just shingles.
